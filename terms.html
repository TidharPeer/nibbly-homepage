Who provides Nibbly

Nibbly is provided by Tidhar Peer. You can contact the developer at tidhar.peer@gmail.com. These terms describe your use of the Nibbly game and its optional in-app purchase. A parent or guardian should manage purchases and support contact for a child.

Free play

Nibbly is free to download. The first 20 campaign levels are available without a purchase, with unlimited retries. There are no advertisements, subscriptions, energy meters, or paid retries.

Full Adventure

Full Adventure is an optional, one-time purchase made through Google Play. It unlocks the rest of the campaign (200 levels in total at the time of this update), future campaign additions when released, Level Workshop, and Custom Levels. Campaign levels open in sequence as you complete earlier levels. Full Adventure is not a subscription.

Google Play shows the price and any applicable taxes before you confirm a purchase and handles payment. A grown-up check appears before purchase options in Nibbly. Purchase access is tied to the Google Play account used to buy it; game progress and custom puzzles are stored on your device and are not restored by restoring the purchase.

Restore a purchase

If you bought Full Adventure and it does not appear, use Restore purchases in Nibbly while signed in to the Google Play account that made the purchase. Restoration needs an internet connection. If it still does not work, contact tidhar.peer@gmail.com for help.

Refunds

To request a refund, use Google Play’s refund request process. Google Play determines eligibility under its refund policies. You can also email tidhar.peer@gmail.com about a purchase issue or refund request. Please include your Google Play order ID if available, but do not send payment-card details. Refunding or revoking Full Adventure may remove access to the paid features.

Support and privacy

For gameplay, purchase, or account questions, email tidhar.peer@gmail.com. For information about local progress, purchase services, and support messages, read the separate Nibbly Privacy Policy.

Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated when Nibbly’s features or purchase arrangements change. The date above identifies this version. Your rights under applicable law remain in place.
